Volunteer

Help Us Grow

As a growing community, we need passionate volunteers to help us create amazing experiences for everyone.

Event Support

  • Help set up and manage social dances
  • Assist with registration and welcoming newcomers
  • Support workshop organization and logistics
  • Help with venue coordination and setup

Community Building

  • Welcome and integrate new dancers
  • Organize social activities and meetups
  • Help maintain our inclusive, friendly atmosphere
  • Support community outreach initiatives

Creative & Media

  • Photography and videography at events
  • Social media content creation and management
  • Design promotional materials and flyers
  • Help maintain our website and online presence

Teaching Support

  • Assist instructors during classes
  • Help with partner rotation and demonstrations
  • Support students and provide encouragement
  • Help maintain a positive learning environment

Join our growing family

Every volunteer helps shape the future of swing dancing in Delhi NCR. Whether you can spare a few hours a month or want to be deeply involved, there's a place for you in our community.
