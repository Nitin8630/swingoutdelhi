Jazz & live music

We don't just dance to the music. We listen to it.

We love the dancing, but we're curious about the music behind it too.

As a community, we head out to hear live jazz bands, discover new musicians and better understand the music we're dancing to. And at our socials, we DJ our way through old swing favourites and new discoveries — always looking for that next song that makes you want to get up and dance.

Because the better you know the music, the more you can play with it.
