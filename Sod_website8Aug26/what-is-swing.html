About · Swing dance & Lindy Hop

What is Swing?

An authentic jazz dance born in 1930s Harlem — joyful, improvised, and deeply musical.

Lindy Hop is the original swing dance. It was created by African American dancers at the Savoy Ballroom in Harlem, New York during the late 1920s and 1930s, dancing to the big-band jazz of Duke Ellington, Count Basie and Chick Webb.

It is a partnered social dance rooted in rhythm, improvisation, and connection. There are no scripted routines on the social floor — every dance is a spontaneous conversation between two people, guided by the pulse of swing jazz music.

Today, Lindy Hop is danced in hundreds of cities worldwide. It is welcoming, joyful, and open to anyone willing to listen to the music and take a first step.

Honouring the roots

Swing Out Delhi acknowledges Lindy Hop as an African American cultural art form. We are grateful to the Black dancers and musicians of Harlem whose creativity, spirit, and resilience gave the world this enduring dance.
