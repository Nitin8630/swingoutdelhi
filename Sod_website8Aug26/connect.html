The Swing World

Building bridges within the global swing dance community.

Building global connections. Swing dance has always been about bringing people together across cultures and borders. As a growing community in Delhi NCR, we're excited to build connections with dancers and communities around the world. Let's swing together and share the universal language of dance!

Visiting Dancers & Teachers

Are you a swing dancer or instructor visiting India? We'd absolutely love to connect with you! Whether you're here for a short visit or an extended stay, our community welcomes international dancers with open arms.

We welcome you to:

  • Join our regular classes and social dances
  • Share your knowledge through workshops
  • Experience Delhi's culture with fellow dancers
  • Build lasting international friendships

Organizing an event?

Are you organizing a swing dance event, workshop, or festival? We'd love to participate! Swing Out Delhi is always excited to connect with other dance communities, share our passion for Lindy Hop, and learn from fellow dancers.
